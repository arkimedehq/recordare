# dev_asking — whom the memory's questions go to (D55 F2b step 4)

Two memories in September 2026: Sara (personal; she tells about her brother Luca's job interview, a bike shop that may
close) and Casa Neri (an entity: Paolo tells about his daughter Giada's swimming race, Marta about her own new diet — both
recognised by their voice, a platform identity —, someone unidentified about Ugo's car). The first run had Paolo and
Marta say who they are in the text; text-declared speakers are not recorded as authors, so the sessions now carry the
voice identity the routing is built on (the gold unchanged). The thought pass (D53) writes the questions; the initiative sweep routes each:
Luca's interview to Sara (Luca never talks to the agent), the bike shop to the agent's tools (a look-up), Giada's race to
Paolo (who told), Marta's diet to Marta; traps — Giada asked about to Marta, Ugo asked about at all, Sara's settled small
talk. Same format as `dataset_dev_initiatives` (`initiative_eval.py`, one checkpoint, no due days). `python build.py`
writes the JSON. Fictional content; a dev set by the developer of the routing (step 4).
